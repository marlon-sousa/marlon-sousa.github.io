[Itinera built together](/series/itinera-built-together/) is out: fourteen articles on how one engineer and a team of AI agents built Itinera, its specification, its conformance suite, its site and its book. It covers the evening the idea was first thought through, the spec-first process designed with an agent and then held to, agents reviewing what other agents proposed, and the decisions that stayed with a person.

The conversations with the agents are quoted exactly as they happened. The series opens with [A year, or fourteen days](article:a-year-or-fourteen-days), and every part after the first opens with a summary of the one before it.
