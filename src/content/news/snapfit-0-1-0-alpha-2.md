The second pre-release of snapfit, on GitHub only, gives every field one `build` setting that chooses how it is built: with a constructor, with `Default`, with a factory you write, or with its type's builder.

[The release on GitHub](https://github.com/marlon-sousa/snapfit-rs/releases/tag/v0.1.0-alpha.2) has the notes.
