snapfit is on crates.io. Version 0.1.0 holds everything the two pre-releases brought, needs Rust 1.85 or newer, and comes with [the Snapfit Book](https://marlon-sousa.com/snapfit-rs/), whose examples and compiler messages are checked on every change, with one edition kept for every release. Async containers and transient dependencies ship behind the `unstable` feature while their shape settles.

[The release on GitHub](https://github.com/marlon-sousa/snapfit-rs/releases/tag/v0.1.0) has the full notes, and `cargo add snapfit` adds it.
