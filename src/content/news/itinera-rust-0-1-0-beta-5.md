---
title: 'Itinera for Rust 0.1.0-beta.5 is released'
date: 2026-10-27
time: '12:37'
project: 'Itinera'
---

The fifth beta of Itinera for Rust breaks nothing written for 0.1.0-beta.4. Its page on crates.io and the opening of its documentation on docs.rs are now written for the people who use it, and it shares its version number, and its conformance cases, with TypeScript's first release.

[The announcement on Itinera's site](https://itinera-dev.org/news/rust-0.1.0-beta.5/) has the details.
